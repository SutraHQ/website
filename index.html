Why SutraHQ?

Most internal developer platforms are built for enterprises with hundreds of services and dedicated platform teams. SutraHQ is designed for startups and engineering teams with 10–50 developers who want simplicity, speed and clarity.

📚 Service Catalog

Know every service, repository, owner and environment in one place.

🚀 Deployments

Track deployments, environments and release history without jumping between tools.

📖 Documentation

Centralize engineering documentation alongside your services.

👥 Ownership

Quickly discover who owns a service and how to contact them.

🔎 Universal Search

Find services, APIs, documentation and repositories instantly.

⚡ Lightweight

Fast setup. Opinionated defaults. No plugin jungle. No platform engineering required.
